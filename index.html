No mystery in the middle

From need to care,
here’s the path.

We keep the process simple to understand and serious where it needs to be. The fund supports verified people, not unverified stories.

A real person is identified with dignity and care.
Medical teams review the need and confirm treatment.
Verified provider support helps keep care moving.
01

A real person is identified

We confirm identity with the patient, family, and care team, using a documented process that respects privacy.

02

The medical need is reviewed

An appropriately qualified healthcare professional or verified provider confirms urgency and treatment required.

03

The cost is checked

We review the provider estimate, verify the requested amount, and assess the case against published allocation rules.

04

Care is paid directly

Approved assistance goes to the hospital, pharmacy, laboratory, or other authorised care provider.

Trust & safety

Care should be
compassionate.
And accountable.

Privacy is not the price of getting help.

Medical records and identity documents are sensitive. Public reporting should show how the fund was used without exposing a patient's private health information.

✓  Medical verification

Cases require identity checks, supporting medical documentation, and confirmation from an appropriately qualified care provider.

 Direct provider payments

Approved assistance is directed to verified healthcare providers against documented treatment costs.

◎  Published allocation rules

Verification does not guarantee funding. Urgency, eligibility, available balance, and fair allocation rules determine approval.

▤  Public financial reporting

Contributions, provider payments, fees, and remaining balances should be reconciled and reported separately.
